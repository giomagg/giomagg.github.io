Hi there!

I am a PhD candidate and Mini-ARC fellow at the Centre d'Étude de la Vie Politique (CEVIPOL) of the Université Libre de Bruxelles. Under the supervision of Prof. Pietro Castelli Gattinara, my thesis uses computational methods to study the discursive mainstreaming of the far-right and its effects on mainstream public discourse.

Research Areas

Political Communication

Far-Right Discourse

Understanding how far-right ideas permeate mainstream political discourse.

Digital Media

Social Media and Politics

Examining how new communication technologies affect politics and public discourse.

Methods

Computational Social Science

Combining network analysis, NLP, and LLMs to scrape and analyze digital trace data.
