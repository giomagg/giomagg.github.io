PhD Research

Ongoing

Discursive Mainstreaming of the Far-Right

My doctoral research investigates how far-right ideas and discourses permeate mainstream public debates. Using computational social science techniques, I analyze large-scale newspaper and digital trace data to understand the mechanisms and effects of far-right mainstreaming on public discourse.

Supervisor: Prof. Pietro Castelli Gattinara

Institution: CEVIPOL, Université Libre de Bruxelles

Funding: Mini-ARC Fellowship
