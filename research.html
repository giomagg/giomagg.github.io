Research

My research is devoted to understanding the structural changes to the public sphere sparked by technological innovation and how we study it. Influenced by the philosophy of Foucault, Latour, and Deleuze, my work has developed around two central axes: (i) the study of digital media, politics and the public sphere; and (ii) meta-research. In both cases, my methodological approach is rooted in computational social science, leveraging SNA and NLP to model (linguistic) interactions between socio-political actors.

I am also interested in mapping and create interactive visualisations of academic literatures using bibliometrics and NLP.
